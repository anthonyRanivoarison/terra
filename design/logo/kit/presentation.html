What we heard

The brief

Free, self-hostable, multi-site web analytics: traffic, goals, revenue attribution and AI crawlers. The mark should stand apart from bar-chart and blue-violet analytics brands, borrow Matomo's confident monoline style, and work as a 16 px favicon.

Concept A

Orbit

A planet (terra) circled by a rising orbit that passes behind it and in front, ending in one data point.

  • Over-under weave gives depth with a single monoline, in the spirit of Matomo
  • The orbit rises at 30°: growth without another bar chart
  • The satellite dot carries over the data point of the previous logo
  • Green is unclaimed in the category (Plausible violet, DataFast coral, Matomo teal)
