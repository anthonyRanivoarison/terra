# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary audience: marketing teams and agencies who run campaigns across several sites and have to answer "which of these actually brought customers?". They come to Terra between campaign reviews and client reports, comparing channels, budgets and landing pages.

## Product Purpose

Terra is a privacy-friendly web analytics product. A team adds a site, pastes one script, and sees where visitors come from and, above all, which channels, pages, campaigns and countries turn into revenue. It also shows when AI assistants and crawlers (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google) read the site. Success is a visitor who creates a free account and adds their first site.

## Positioning

Revenue, not just visits: Terra connects every visit to the money it brings, so teams invest in the channels that sell instead of the ones that only send traffic.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: create a free account. Secondary CTA: see the live demo.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: Terra shows which channels bring revenue, not just visitors.
- Belief ladder: traffic numbers alone mislead spend; Terra ties each visit to the revenue it produces; setup is one script and takes a minute; it is free to start.
- Proof on hand: none yet. Show the product itself (demo figures, the install snippet, AI crawler activity) rather than invented testimonials or logos.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, futuristic. Speaks like a high-end measuring instrument: confident, quiet, exact. Numbers and motion carry the excitement; the copy stays short and plain.

## Anti-references

- A generic SaaS template: screenshot hero, a grid of six feature cards, a strip of client logos.
- The Google Analytics look: dense, gray, intimidating.
- Neon cyberpunk: loud futurism, purple gradients, glow everywhere.

## Design Principles

- Practice what you preach: show real-looking analytics in motion instead of describing them.
- Money is the hero: every section leads back to revenue, not vanity traffic.
- Calm precision: generous space, exact numbers, restrained motion that feels engineered.
- One script, one minute: make setup feel effortless and visible.
